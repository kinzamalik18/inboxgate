# Kestrel Studio (fictional agency used for this demo)

Kestrel Studio is a six-person web studio. We design and build websites and web apps for small and mid-sized businesses.

## Services and starting prices
- Shopify stores: new builds from $6,000, redesigns from $4,000. Typical timeline 6 to 10 weeks.
- WordPress marketing sites: from $3,500. Typical timeline 4 to 6 weeks.
- Custom web apps (React and Python): priced after a paid discovery week, which costs $1,500.
- Care plans for sites we built or inherited: $250 per month, including updates, backups and 3 hours of small changes.
- We don't do SEO campaigns, paid ads or logo-only branding.

## How new projects start
New leads get a 30-minute intro call. We send a proposal within 3 business days of the call. Our earliest start date is usually 3 to 4 weeks out.

## Support
Care plan clients get replies within 4 business hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 UK time. Site outages for care plan clients are handled the same day, including weekends.

## Billing
Invoices are due in 14 days. We accept bank transfer and card. Billing questions go to the billing team. Refunds are handled case by case by the founders, so the reply should never promise a refund.

## Tone
Friendly, direct, no jargon. Sign replies as "The Kestrel Studio team".
